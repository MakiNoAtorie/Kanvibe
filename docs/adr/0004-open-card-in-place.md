# The open Card expands in place

A Card's fields (Labels, description, Checklist, actions, times) show by expanding the selected Card in place inside its Column, instead of in a 400px panel on the right or in a modal. The panel squeezed the Board on every open while mostly showing empty space, and a modal would hide the Board that gives the Card its context; in place, the Board keeps its full width. (Amended 2026-10-28: the open Card no longer follows the arrow keys or opens on a click; Enter, a double-click or an edit shortcut opens one. Inside the open Card, Up/Down highlight its parts, F2 edits the highlighted one and Enter closes it; Left/Right do nothing.) The cost we accepted: a 280px Column is narrow for long descriptions and wide Checklists. If that proves cramped, the follow-up is to widen the open Card's Column while it is open, not to bring back the panel.
